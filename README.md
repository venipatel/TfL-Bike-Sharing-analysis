# TfL-Bike-Sharing-analysis
Analysed Tfl bike journey data using Power BI to explore travel patterns, peak hours, popular stations and journey durations.
I worked with TfL bike journey data in Power BI to understand how people use bike across London.
I found some popular stations are used the most, when bike demand is highest, and how travel patterns change between weekdays and weekends.

# Tools Used
Power BI, Power Query, DAX.
# What I Found
1) Analysed around 2.47 million bike journeys.
2) On weekdays, the busiest hours are 7-9 AM and 4-6 PM.
3) On weekends, bike usage is highest between 11 AM and 6 PM.
4) Hyde Park Corner and Waterloo are the most popular stations.
5) Black Lion Gate and soho square have the lowest numbers of journeys.
6) Around 4.82% of journeys were identified as outliers.

# My Recommendations
1) Make more bikes available during peak hours.
2) Focus on most popular stations like Hayde Park Corner and Waterloo.
3) Plan bike distribution differently for weekdays and weekend.
4) Check unusually long journeys to understand whether they are genuine trips, delayed returns or data errors.

 # What i learned
 This project helped me practise data cleaning, working with large datasets, creating Power BI dashboards and finding useful insights from data and making recommendations.
